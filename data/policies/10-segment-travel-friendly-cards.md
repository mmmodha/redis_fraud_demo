# Customer-Segment Guidance — Travel-Friendly Cards

**Document type:** Customer-experience guidance
**Applies to:** Cards on the Voyager, Voyager Plus and Voyager Signature
products, plus any card whose primary cardholder has opted into the
"frequent traveller" profile.

## Product positioning

The travel-friendly card range is marketed on the explicit promise that
the card "just works abroad". This promise has direct fraud-policy
consequences: a foreign-travel decline on a Voyager card is treated as a
Severity 2 customer incident and reviewed by the Fraud Operations
Committee within 5 business days.

## Authorisation defaults

For cards in this segment:

- The foreign-travel approval window opens **automatically** for 14 days
  on either side of any confirmed airline or hotel booking.
- Foreign-currency transactions under USD 500 are approved without
  step-up regardless of geography, provided no other risk multiplier
  fires.
- Currency-conversion fees are waived; this is a product feature, not a
  fraud control, but it materially changes spending patterns and the
  feature store should reflect it (cardholders use the card more
  intensively abroad).
- Lost-card replacement abroad is delivered within 24 hours in most
  destinations.

## Common false-positive sources to avoid

The following patterns produce the most complaints from this segment and
should not by themselves trigger a step-up:

- First transaction after landing (typically at an airport currency
  exchange or transit kiosk).
- High-velocity low-value charges at hotel front desks (incidentals).
- Multiple foreign-currency conversions on the same calendar day.

## Cross-references

- Foreign-travel policy: open travel windows from agent memory as well as
  bookings.
- Velocity policy: travel-friendly cards inherit the premium-segment
  thresholds when the cardholder is inside an open travel window.
